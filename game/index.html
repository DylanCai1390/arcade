Level 1 · Mini Crossword

Mini Crossword

Ten clues, twenty-three squares. Click or tab into a square, type a letter, and press Tab to move on.

Puzzle grid

Fill every square to clear the level. Level clear! Every letter is correct.

Clues

Across

  1. Rating earned for clearing a level, up to three at a time (4)
  2. Top tier, like the initials on a high-score table (5)
  3. Luxury vacation rental (5)
  4. Perfect, like a speedrunner’s route (5)
  5. “___ is more” (a minimalist design motto) (4)

Down

  1. Playground chute, or one page of a presentation (5)
  2. Numbered squares you merge in 2048 (5)
  3. Book of maps, or one image that packs every sprite in a game (5)
  4. Not virtual (4)
  5. Like most final bosses (4)

How to play

  • Click or tap a white square, or press Tab to move into the grid, then type a letter.
  • Press Tab to go to the next square and Shift + Tab to go back. The across word you are in lights up, along with both clues for that square.
  • Turn on Show mistakes to mark wrong letters with a red slash as you type.
  • Fill every square correctly and the board lights up to tell you the level is clear.
